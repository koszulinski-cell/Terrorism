An interactive map of terrorism incidents in the United States.

About this map

Each point on the map represents a recorded terrorism incident in the United States contained in the underlying dataset. Select a point to see the available information about that incident.

The map is intended as a research and educational visualization. The presence of an incident in the dataset does not by itself establish a particular legal classification or motive.
