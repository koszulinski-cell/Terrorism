About this dataset

This visualization displays documented attacks in the United States using a publicly accessible geographic layer derived from the University of Maryland START Global Terrorism Database.

The underlying dataset records incidents and associated geographic, casualty and other event information. The visualization does not independently determine whether an incident legally constitutes domestic terrorism.

Inclusion in the underlying dataset should not be interpreted as a legal finding or criminal conviction.

Source: University of Maryland START / Global Terrorism Database-derived public geographic dataset.
