About this project

This project presents a compiled map of documented terrorism incidents in the United States. The classification of an event as terrorism comes from the underlying source dataset and does not necessarily mean that a particular person was legally convicted of terrorism.

The historical terrorism data are derived from the Global Terrorism Database (GTD), maintained by the National Consortium for the Study of Terrorism and Responses to Terrorism (START) at the University of Maryland.

GTD contains incident-level information including date, location and casualties. Its current published series covers 1970–2020, with a separate partial 2021 release.

Important: this is a research and educational visualization, not an official government classification system.

Sources: START / Global Terrorism Database and publicly available GTD-derived data. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
