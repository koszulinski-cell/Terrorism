About this map

This project presents publicly documented incidents associated with terrorism and political violence in the United States. Different organizations use different definitions and inclusion criteria. An incident appearing on this map does not necessarily mean that every government agency, court, researcher, or other organization formally classified it as terrorism or political violence.

The terrorism dataset used by this project is based on the U.S. Terrorism Incidents Dataset maintained by the Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS).

Political-violence data should be treated as a separate category. ACLED defines political violence as the use of force by a group with a political purpose or motivation.

This project is intended for research, education, journalism, and public information.
